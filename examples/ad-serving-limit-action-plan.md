# Ad serving limit action plan

A dated plan for an account under an ad serving limit. Copy
this structure, fill it with the account's own facts, and
keep it updated weekly until the Policy Center clears the
issue. Do not send this plan to Google. It is a working
log, not an appeal.

Read the method in
`references/24-ad-serving-limits-and-enforcement-levels.md`
and the traffic defense method in
`references/25-invalid-traffic-defense.md`.

## Rules for this log

- Dates on every entry. A log without dates is a story.
- Facts only. "Paused X on 4 March" beats "fixed traffic".
- One change at a time after clearance. If the limit
  returns, the log must show which change came last.
