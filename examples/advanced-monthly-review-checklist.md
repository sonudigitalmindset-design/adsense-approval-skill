# Advanced monthly review checklist

One focused hour a month for an approved account. Work top
to bottom. Any item that fails becomes a dated task, not a
mental note.

Read the reporting method in
`references/26-advanced-reporting-and-experiments.md` and
the enforcement ladder in
`references/24-ad-serving-limits-and-enforcement-levels.md`.

## Policy Center

- No new page, site, or account level issues.
- Every open issue has a fix date before its deadline.
- Every fixed issue shows as resolved, not merely edited.

## Reports, segmented

- Total earnings and finalised earnings compared with last
  month and the same month last year.
- Reports segmented by page URL. High impression low RPM
  pages noted, high CTR pages investigated.
- Reports segmented by country. Country mix shifts noted
  and explained by real traffic, not assumed.
- Reports segmented by device. Mobile behaviour checked on
  a phone sized view for the top pages.
- Deductions checked in the transactions view. Any
  deduction investigated the same week.

## Traffic

- Traffic sources listed and named. No unnamed source.
- Analytics checked for spikes, single-source surges, and
  engagement collapse.
- New promotion channels reviewed for behaviour before
  being trusted.
- Traffic log updated with the month's changes.

## Blocking controls

- Blocked categories and advertiser URLs reviewed.
- Every block still has a brand or audience reason.
- Blocks set in a panic and never reviewed are removed or
  justified in writing.

## Technical checks

- ads.txt fetched live and compared with the account line.
- Consent banner tested as a new visitor, including the
  decline path.
- One CMP active. No second consent plugin running.
- Ad code unmodified on every template.
- No auto-refresh, auto-redirect, or preset-time ad gate.
- Crawler access open. robots.txt checked after any
  migration or firewall change.
- Top pages checked on a phone sized view for layout
  shift, covered content, and ads near tap targets.

## Content and trust pages

- New pages published this month meet the quality bar in
  `references/09-content-quality-guide.md`.
- No thin, copied, or unedited generated page added.
- About, Contact, and Privacy pages current, and the
  Privacy disclosure still matches the live consent setup.
- Comments moderated. Spam, links to violating content,
  and encouraged clicks removed.

## Experiments and changes

- One experiment running at most, one variable changed.
- Last experiment's result recorded with dates and traffic
  level before the next test starts.
- Ad load and format toggles unchanged unless an experiment
  or a policy fix required the change.

## Verification and payments

- Payments page shows no holds.
- Identity and address verification complete.
- Payment method valid, primary, and name-matched.
- Tax information current, and resubmitted after any name
  change.
- Balance and threshold position noted ahead of the cycle.
