## How to verify a rule in 2 minutes

1. Open the AdSense Help Center and search for the exact topic.
2. Read the page, not a blog summary of it.
3. Note the date you read it and the exact wording.
4. If the page contradicts this skill, the page wins. Update the skill
   and record the change in `CHANGELOG.md`.
