# Mistakes encyclopedia

Fifty mistakes that get sites rejected or accounts closed, grouped
by stage. Each entry states the mistake, why it hurts, and the fix.
Severity: blocker means fix before applying, risk means it can grow
into enforcement, habit means it costs revenue or trust over time.

## Before applying

1. Applying with a nearly empty site. A reviewer sees a site with
   almost nothing to judge, so the decision is usually low value
   content. Fix: publish a body of finished, useful pages first.
   Blocker. See `08-pre-apply-checklist.md`.
2. Applying with demo content still live. Default posts, sample
   pages, and lorem ipsum tell the reviewer the site is unfinished.
   Fix: delete every demo item. Blocker.
3. Applying while the site is under construction. A coming soon page
   or half-built layout cannot pass a whole-site review. Fix: finish
   the site first. Blocker.
4. Applying with broken navigation. Menus that lead nowhere, dead
   footer links, and 404 pages break the review. Fix: click every
   link as a stranger. Blocker.
5. Applying with no About page. The reviewer cannot tell who runs
   the site. Fix: publish a real About page in your own words.
   Blocker.
6. Applying with no Contact page. No way to reach the owner looks
   untrustworthy. Fix: publish a contact page with a working form or
   address. Blocker.
7. Applying with no Privacy Policy. The advertising disclosure is
   required. Fix: publish a Privacy Policy that names third-party
   advertising cookies. Blocker. See `18-consent-and-privacy-deep-dive.md`.
8. Copying a Privacy Policy or About page word for word. Copied
   trust pages can read as fake and may not describe your site.
   Fix: write them for your own site. Risk.
9. Applying from a second account to dodge a rejection. Duplicate
   accounts for the same payee are a policy problem, not a shortcut.
   Fix: fix the first application. Blocker.
10. Applying with a site you do not control. You need access to the
    HTML to place verification code. Fix: get control first or do
    not apply. Blocker.
11. Applying the day the domain is registered with no history.
    There is no official domain age rule, but a brand-new empty site
    still fails on content. Fix: build content and let the site
    settle. Risk.
12. Removing the verification code during review. The site can
    become unverified. Fix: leave the code in place until the review
    ends. Blocker.

## Content mistakes

13. Publishing thin pages that answer nothing. A paragraph of
    generic text per post is the classic low value content signal.
    Fix: merge, expand, or delete. Blocker.
14. Copying content from other sites. Copied text is a copyright
    and quality violation. Fix: write original work and remove
    copies. Blocker.
15. Spinning or lightly rewriting other people's articles. Changed
    words with the same structure is still unoriginal. Fix: research
    and write from your own outline. Blocker.
16. Publishing raw AI output without editing. Unedited generated
    text is often generic, repetitive, and wrong on facts. Fix:
    edit hard, verify facts, add your own examples. Risk. See
    `09-content-quality-guide.md`.
17. Publishing AI content with invented facts. Fake statistics,
    fake quotes, and fake citations destroy trust. Fix: verify every
    number against a real source or remove it. Risk.
18. Stuffing keywords into titles and text. Keyword stuffing reads
    as spam and hurts readers. Fix: write for the question, not the
    keyword count. Risk.
19. Writing health, money, or legal advice with no sourcing. These
    topics get extra scrutiny. Fix: cite credible sources and add a
    disclaimer. Risk.
20. Claiming personal experience you do not have. Fake reviews and
    fake test stories are deceptive. Fix: only claim what you
    tested, or write neutral guidance. Risk.
21. Leaving empty categories and tag pages indexed. Thin archive
    pages count in a whole-site review. Fix: fill them, noindex
    them, or remove them. Risk.
22. Keeping scraped feeds, auto-generated location pages, or
    doorway pages. Mass-produced low value pages are a rejection
    cause. Fix: delete them. Blocker.
23. Using copyrighted images, videos, or text without permission.
    Copyright violations can remove ads from pages or the account.
    Fix: use your own media or properly licensed media. Blocker.
24. Publishing prohibited content anywhere on the site. One bad
    page can affect the whole review. Fix: remove it entirely, not
    just the ads. Blocker. See `03-publisher-policies.md`.

## Traffic and click mistakes

25. Clicking your own ads. Self-clicks are invalid traffic. Fix:
    never click, and keep friends and family from clicking too.
    Blocker. See `05-program-policies-ads-traffic.md`.
26. Asking visitors to click ads. Any encouragement to click,
    including hints and arrows, is a violation. Fix: remove every
    such prompt. Blocker.
27. Buying traffic from click exchanges or cheap traffic sellers.
    Paid junk traffic is invalid traffic. Fix: stop it and build
    real traffic. Blocker.
28. Running traffic bots or auto-refresh scripts. Automated
    impressions are invalid traffic. Fix: remove them. Blocker.
29. Ignoring a sudden traffic spike. A spike from an unknown source
    can be an attack or junk traffic. Fix: check referrers, block
    bad sources, and report what you find. Risk.
30. Sending paid social traffic straight to ad-heavy pages with no
    content value. Low intent traffic plus heavy ads looks like
    arbitrage. Fix: send paid traffic to genuinely useful pages.
    Risk.
31. Using popups or misleading buttons that cause accidental
    clicks. Accidental clicks are invalid traffic. Fix: keep ads
    away from navigation and download controls. Blocker.
32. Sharing your site in click-for-click groups. Coordinated clicks
    are invalid traffic. Fix: leave the groups. Blocker.

## Ad placement mistakes

33. Placing ads under misleading headings such as "resources" or
    "helpful links". Ads must not be disguised as content or
    navigation. Fix: label ad areas plainly. Blocker.
34. Placing ads next to download or play buttons. Users click the
    ad by mistake. Fix: move ads away from action buttons. Blocker.
35. Covering content with ads or popups. Content must stay readable
    and reachable. Fix: keep ads out of the reading path. Blocker.
36. Showing more ads than content on a page. Ad-heavy pages fail
    the value test. Fix: keep content clearly dominant. Risk.
37. Using floating or sticky ads that hide content or cannot be
    dismissed. Undismissable overlays are a placement violation.
    Fix: use formats that dismiss cleanly. Risk.
38. Putting ads on error pages, login pages, or thank-you pages.
    Ads belong on real content pages. Fix: exclude those pages.
    Risk.
39. Putting ads on pages with prohibited content. Ads on violating
    pages must be removed along with the content. Fix: remove the
    content. Blocker.
40. Modifying the ad code. Edited code can break serving or policy
    compliance. Fix: use the code exactly as issued. Blocker.

## Privacy and consent mistakes

41. No consent banner for EEA, UK, or Swiss visitors. A certified
    CMP is required for that traffic. Fix: publish a Funding
    Choices message or another certified CMP. Blocker for that
    traffic. See `18-consent-and-privacy-deep-dive.md`.
42. A homemade cookie banner claimed as a certified CMP. Only
    certified CMPs meet the requirement. Fix: use a certified one
    and do not claim certification you do not have. Risk.
43. Loading ad code before consent is collected. Tracking must
    wait for the visitor's choice in regulated regions. Fix: gate
    the code behind the CMP. Risk.
44. A Privacy Policy that never mentions advertising cookies.
    Visitors must be told third parties serve and measure ads.
    Fix: add the disclosure. Blocker.
45. Collecting emails or personal data with no disclosure. Any
    data collection needs an honest explanation. Fix: disclose
    what you collect and why. Risk.

## Account and payment mistakes

46. Using a nickname or a name that does not match your ID. Name
    mismatches block verification and payments. Fix: use your exact
    legal name everywhere. Blocker. See
    `16-verification-and-payments-troubleshooting.md`.
47. Entering an address that cannot receive post. The PIN letter
    goes to that address. Fix: use a real postal address. Blocker.
48. Ignoring identity verification deadlines. Missed deadlines stop
    ad serving. Fix: complete verification inside the shown window.
    Blocker.
49. Uploading blurry or glare-covered ID photos. Unreadable
    documents waste limited attempts. Fix: shoot in flat light with
    all details readable. Risk.
50. Skipping tax information. Missing tax info holds payments and
    can raise withholding. Fix: submit it early and accurately.
    Blocker.
51. Letting the Policy Center sit unread. A page-level flag grows
    when ignored. Fix: check it weekly and fix flags fast. Habit.
    See `15-policy-center-and-appeals.md`.
52. Over-blocking ad categories to chase higher earnings. Blocking
    removes bidders and usually lowers revenue. Fix: block only for
    brand safety with evidence. Habit. See
    `14-optimization-and-revenue.md`.
