# Learning paths

Read in order for your level. Each level lists what to read, what
to be able to do, and the check that proves it. The whole skill
runs from zero to advanced with no gap. Skip a level only when you
can already pass its check.

## Level 0: Complete beginner

You have never used AdSense.

Read:

- `10-glossary.md` for the vocabulary.
- `01-history-timeline.md` for how AdSense began and changed.
- `02-eligibility-and-application.md` for what Google requires
  and the myths to ignore.

Be able to:

- Explain what AdSense is in two sentences.
- Name the four eligibility checks.
- Name three myths that are not official rules.

Check: explain to a friend why a site with copied content and no
About page will not be approved.

## Level 1: Preparing a first site

You own a site and want to apply.

Read:

- `09-content-quality-guide.md` for what good content looks like.
- `03-publisher-policies.md` and `04-publisher-restrictions.md`
  for what can and cannot carry ads.
- `12-mistakes-encyclopedia.md` for the errors to avoid.
- `08-pre-apply-checklist.md` for the final check.

Be able to:

- Audit a site page by page and mark each page keep, improve,
  merge, or delete.
- Write About, Contact, and Privacy pages in your own words.
- Run `scripts/site_audit.py` and fix every blocker.

Check: complete the pre-apply checklist with zero blockers and a
written reason for each item.

## Level 2: Applying and waiting

You have applied or are about to.

Read:

- `02-eligibility-and-application.md` again for the exact steps.
- `19-platform-setup-guides.md` for code placement and ads.txt.
- `18-consent-and-privacy-deep-dive.md` for the CMP setup.
- `07-rejection-reasons-and-fixes.md` so a rejection does not
  panic you.

Be able to:

- Place verification code, publish ads.txt, and confirm both live.
- Publish a certified CMP message for regulated traffic.
- Read a rejection notice and match it to a fix plan.

Check: the live site shows the code, the ads.txt file, and the CMP
banner, all verified in your own browser.

## Level 3: Approved and running ads

Ads are serving.

Read:

- `05-program-policies-ads-traffic.md` for the rules that close
  accounts.
- `13-ad-formats-and-placement.md` for formats and placement.
- `15-policy-center-and-appeals.md` for reading enforcement.
- `20-troubleshooting-index.md` for the first response to any
  symptom.

Be able to:

- Set up Auto ads with sensible exclusions and preview them on a
  phone.
- Read the Policy Center and fix a page-level flag.
- Spot a suspicious traffic spike and record what you found.

Check: one month with no Policy Center flags and a written log of
traffic sources checked weekly.

## Level 4: Optimization

The account is stable and you want more from it.

Read:

- `14-optimization-and-revenue.md` for metrics and experiments.
- `21-traffic-and-seo-basics.md` for durable traffic growth.
- `13-ad-formats-and-placement.md` again for layout testing.

Be able to:

- Read reports by page, country, device, and unit.
- Run one clean experiment and judge it by total earnings.
- Explain why over-blocking categories usually lowers revenue.

Check: one completed experiment with a written result and a kept
or rolled-back decision.

## Level 5: Advanced and multi-product

You manage more than one site or product, or you handle appeals
and payments.

Read:

- `16-verification-and-payments-troubleshooting.md` for identity,
  PIN, tax, and holds.
- `17-youtube-and-other-products.md` for YPP, Search, and Ad
  Manager.
- `06-terms-and-payments.md` for the full Terms and payment
  story.
- `11-official-sources.md` and recheck every live page.

Be able to:

- Complete identity and PIN verification without wasting
  attempts.
- Submit tax information correctly for your country.
- Write a fact-based appeal with evidence, once, carefully.
- Explain the differences between AdSense for Content, AdSense
  for YouTube, AdSense for Search, AdMob, and Ad Manager.

Check: a second site or product set up cleanly with no policy
flags in its first month, and a written incident record for any
enforcement handled.

## Frequently asked questions

- How many posts do I need? Google publishes no minimum. Apply
  when the site is a finished body of useful content, not at a
  magic number.
- How long does review take? Usually a few days, sometimes 2 to 4
  weeks.
- Can I apply with a free subdomain? Ownership and control matter.
  A custom domain is strongly preferred and far more credible.
- Can I have two AdSense accounts? No, one account per payee.
- Can I click my own ads to test them? No, never. Use preview
  tools and reports instead.
- Does AI content get rejected? Unedited, generic, or invented
  content fails on quality. Edited, verified, genuinely useful
  content is judged on its value, not its tool.
- Do I need traffic before applying? No official minimum exists.
  Real traffic helps the review see a live site, but bought
  traffic hurts.
- What if my site is in a restricted category? Ads may be limited
  on that content. Read `04-publisher-restrictions.md` before
  building around it.
- Can I reapply after rejection? Yes, after real fixes. Do not
  reapply with no changes.
- Can I use AdSense with other ad networks? Yes, where the other
  network's ads comply with AdSense placement rules and the page
  stays content-dominant.
- Why are my earnings zero with traffic? Check site status, Policy
  Center, ad code on live pages, and whether ads are actually
  rendering in a private window.
- When do I get paid? After the balance reaches the threshold and
  all holds clear, in the 21st to 26th window. See
  `16-verification-and-payments-troubleshooting.md`.
- What happens to my balance if the account is closed? The Terms
  govern it. See `06-terms-and-payments.md`. Do not assume a
  payout.
- Is there an AdSense phone number? No verified public phone
  number is published by Google. Use the account's support options
  and the Help Community.
