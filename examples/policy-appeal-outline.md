# Policy appeal outline

A structure for an invalid traffic appeal. Fill it with your own
facts, dates, and numbers. Do not copy sample wording. Google asks
for detailed, specific information, and generic text reads as
generic. See `references/15-policy-center-and-appeals.md` before
submitting. Submit once, carefully.

## 1. Account identification

- Name on the account: [exact name]
- Publisher ID: [pub- ID from the account]
- Contact email: [email tied to the disabled account]
- Date of the disablement notice: [date]
- Exact reason stated in the notice: [quote it]

## 2. What happened, in dated order

- [Date]: [what changed on the site or in traffic]
- [Date]: [when the spike or anomaly started, with numbers]
- [Date]: [when you first noticed it, and how]
- [Date]: [what you did immediately]

Write only what you can show from logs, reports, or records. If a
cause is unknown, say so plainly instead of guessing.

## 3. Traffic evidence

- Traffic sources in the affected period: [search, social,
  direct, referral, with shares]
- Suspicious referrers found: [list with dates and volumes]
- Suspicious IP ranges or patterns: [list with evidence]
- Paid traffic used in the period: [none, or full details of each
  campaign, vendor, and date range]
- Changes to placements in the period: [what moved, when]

Attach or paste the log and report extracts the form asks for.
Remove nothing that looks bad. Hiding a fact hurts more than
admitting it.

## 4. Honest statement of responsibility

- Did you, your team, or anyone you are responsible for click ads,
  encourage clicks, or buy traffic? Answer truthfully.
- If yes: state what happened, why, and that it has stopped.
- If no: state what evidence supports that, such as logs showing
  the source was external.

## 5. Changes already made

- [Change]: [date made, with URL or setting]
- [Change]: [date made, with URL or setting]
- [Change]: [date made, with URL or setting]

Only list changes that are actually live. Planned changes go in
the next section.

## 6. Changes you will maintain

- Weekly traffic source review: [who checks, what they check]
- Placement review: [what was fixed to prevent accidental clicks]
- Paid traffic policy: [what is now banned, in writing]
- Monitoring: [tools and alerts in place]

## 7. What you are asking for

- A review of the disablement decision based on the evidence
  above.
- One sentence stating the account now complies and the cause is
  fixed. No promises about future earnings. No blame toward
  Google.

## Before you submit

- Every fact is true and checkable.
- Every date and number comes from a record, not memory.
- No copied template text remains.
- The form fields are filled completely, including the traffic log
  data field.
- You have saved a copy of the full submission with the date.
