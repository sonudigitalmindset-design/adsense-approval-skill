# YouTube and other AdSense products

AdSense is a family of products. The rules, accounts, and revenue
shares differ between them. Do not mix them up. The YouTube
thresholds below were verified against the official YouTube Help
page (answer 72851) in November 2026.

## AdSense for Content (websites)

- The main product this skill covers: ads on websites you own.
- Approval is a whole-site review. See
  `02-eligibility-and-application.md`.
- Per-impression payment since early 2024. Publishers receive 80
  percent after the buy-side fee, about 68 percent overall when the
  buyer is Google Ads.

## AdSense for YouTube

- Monetisation on YouTube runs through the YouTube Partner Program
  (YPP), with an AdSense for YouTube account linked to the channel.
- Full YPP eligibility, from the official Help page: 1,000
  subscribers plus either 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last
  12 months or 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90
  days. Shorts views in the Shorts Feed do not count toward the
  4,000 watch hours.
- The channel must also follow the YouTube channel monetisation
  policies, be in a country where YPP is available, have no active
  Community Guidelines strikes, and have one active AdSense for
  YouTube account linked.
- An expanded YPP tier gives earlier access to fan funding and
  Shopping features in available countries. Check the live Help
  page for its lower thresholds. Do not quote them from memory.
- YPP review checks the channel as a whole, including reused
  content. Uploading other people's videos, compilations with no
  added value, and mass-produced templated videos commonly fail
  review.
- Create the AdSense for YouTube account inside YouTube Studio.
  Do not create a separate AdSense for Content account and try to
  link it. If an account already exists, Studio offers to link it.
- A disabled AdSense for YouTube account blocks YPP monetisation
  even when the channel meets every other requirement. Fix the
  AdSense account first. See
  `15-policy-center-and-appeals.md`.
- AdSense for YouTube earnings are subject to US tax withholding
  on US-viewer revenue. See
  `16-verification-and-payments-troubleshooting.md`.
- One payee, one AdSense account. The same person cannot hold a
  separate AdSense for Content account and an AdSense for YouTube
  account under the same payee details to work around a problem.

## AdSense for Search

- A Google powered search box for a site, with ads on the results
  pages.
- Publishers receive 51 percent of the revenue. This share did not
  change in the 2024 update.
- Search ads follow the same policy layers as content ads, plus
  search-specific rules. Do not place search boxes where they
  mislead users into clicking ads.
- Setup is a search engine unit in the AdSense account. See
  `13-ad-formats-and-placement.md`.

## AdSense for Games and other surfaces

- Google offers ad products for games and apps under separate
  names and signups, including AdMob for mobile apps and AdSense
  for Games for web games. Each has its own Help pages, formats,
  and policies.
- Do not apply website AdSense rules to an app or a game. Read
  that product's own documentation first.

## Google Ad Manager

- Ad Manager is the larger publisher platform for sites that sell
  direct ads alongside programmatic demand. It is not AdSense.
- Some publishers outgrow AdSense and move to Ad Manager, often
  through a partner. The move changes reporting, controls, and
  support. It is not an approval shortcut.
- Ad Manager accounts can be linked to AdSense for YouTube in
  multi-channel network setups. That is an advanced arrangement
  with its own contracts. Do not improvise it.

## What is not AdSense

- Google Ads is the advertiser side. Buying Google Ads does not
  help an AdSense application.
- YouTube channel memberships, Super Chat, and Shopping are YPP
  features, not AdSense products. They have their own eligibility.
- Third-party ad networks (for example Mediavine or Raptive) are
  separate companies with their own requirements. They often run
  Google demand through their own accounts. Compare them on
  contract terms, not on promised RPM figures.

## Choosing the right product

- A website or blog: AdSense for Content.
- A YouTube channel: YPP plus AdSense for YouTube.
- A site search box: AdSense for Search, alongside or instead of
  content ads.
- A mobile app: AdMob.
- A large site with direct sales: Google Ad Manager, usually with
  professional help.
