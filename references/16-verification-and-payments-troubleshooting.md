# Verification and payments troubleshooting

Identity verification, address PIN verification, payment methods,
tax information, and payment holds. Deadlines and thresholds shown
in the account win over anything written here. Figures marked
reported come from secondary sources checked against Help pages in
October 2026, not from a direct read of the live page.

## The five-point name match

Verification fails most often on names. Before submitting anything,
make the same legal name appear, spelled the same way, in:

1. The Google Account profile name.
2. The Google Payments profile payee name.
3. The government photo ID.
4. The bank account name.
5. The tax information form.

A nickname, a shortened name, a changed surname spelling, or a
different name order can each block verification. Fix the payments
profile name before submitting documents, since the name may be
hard or impossible to change after verification succeeds.

## Identity verification

Status: OFFICIAL. The process below was read on the live
AdSense Help page "Verify your identity using online
verification" on 17 November 2026:
https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/16354192?hl=en
The identity section of this file no longer relies on
secondary reports. The rest of the file keeps its
October 2026 labels.

- Google asks for identity verification when the account
  reaches the verification threshold. The request appears
  in the account under Payments and Verification. From
  June 2026, identity verification may also be required
  before the account can start showing ads at all.
- The deadline is 45 days from the date Google first asks
  for verification. After 45 days without completion,
  Google stops showing ads on the pages. For a new
  account, activation pauses instead.
- Online verification is only available in certain
  locations. Where it is available, the process takes
  5 to 10 minutes and has two parts: an image of a
  current government-issued photo ID, and a video selfie
  for a face match.
- Supported document types are a passport, a driver's
  license, and a national identity card. The document
  must come from the country in the payments profile.
  An ID from another country fails even when the name
  matches, and the payment country cannot be changed.
- Document photo tips from the official page: use the
  same full name in all documents, use a current
  document that is not expired, show the entire
  document with the photo readable and in focus, and
  keep the photo free of reflections and glare.
  Incomplete, obstructed, dark, or blurry photos are
  not accepted.
- Video selfie tips from the official page: keep the
  face visible and centered, be the only person in the
  video, keep the camera at a reasonable distance, and
  turn the head slowly and fully when prompted.
- Do not upload anything that is not an ID. The official
  page warns that uploading documents or photos other
  than the ID may result in account suspension. Bank
  statements and screenshots are not identity documents.
- Publishers can opt out of the biometric face match.
  Opting out sends the documents to a manual review
  that takes 2 to 3 business days.
- Attempts are limited. A retry is allowed when the
  documents were blurry or unreadable. Do not resubmit
  the same failing document. Fix the cause first, then
  submit once with a clean document.
- If attempts run out, Google points to the Identity
  verification troubleshooter and then a contact form.
  Follow that path instead of creating a new account.
- Common rejection causes: name mismatch, wrong
  document country, glare or blur, expired document,
  cropped or cut-off document, and a document type
  Google does not accept for that country.
- Google may periodically re-review previously approved
  accounts or re-evaluate verification status based on
  new risk and safety signals, such as a connection to
  a terminated account. If an account fails identity
  verification on re-review, ads stop showing and
  earnings are placed on hold.
- In some countries, passing online identity
  verification removes the separate address verification
  step. The account shows which steps remain.

## Address verification and the PIN

- When earnings reach the verification threshold, commonly 10 US
  dollars or the local equivalent, Google mails a 6-digit PIN to
  the payment address.
- The letter usually takes 2 to 4 weeks, sometimes longer by
  country. Payments stay on hold until the PIN is entered.
- If the letter does not arrive after about 3 weeks, request a new
  PIN from the account. Requests are limited, commonly reported as
  3 in total.
- Enter the PIN carefully. Wrong entries are limited. If no PIN
  arrives after the allowed requests, the account may offer
  alternative verification, such as an address document.
- The PIN entry window is commonly reported as 4 months from the
  first mailing. The account shows the real deadline.
- Check the address before requesting another PIN. A wrong address
  wastes a request. The address must be able to receive ordinary
  post.

## Payment methods

- Add the payment method in the payments profile before the
  payment threshold is reached, so nothing blocks the first payout.
- The bank account name must match the payee name. Mismatches get
  payments returned by the bank.
- Check the account number and SWIFT or BIC code with the bank,
  not from memory. A returned payment restarts the waiting cycle.
- Available payment methods vary by country. In India, wire
  transfer is reported as the only AdSense payment method. Check
  the account for the methods offered in your country.
- The bank must be in the same country as the payments address in
  most cases. Confirm this in the account before adding a foreign
  bank.
- Update payment details before the monthly cutoff if anything
  changed. The cutoff is commonly reported as the 20th of the
  month for that month's payment cycle.

## Tax information

- Every publisher must submit tax information in the payments
  profile. Individuals outside the US generally use Form W-8BEN.
  Businesses generally use Form W-8BEN-E. US publishers use a
  different form. The account guides the choice.
- Without tax information, Google may withhold up to 24 percent of
  worldwide YouTube earnings. With the form, withholding applies
  only to earnings from US viewers, at a rate set by treaty, from
  0 to 30 percent.
- The India-US treaty rate for royalties is reported as 15
  percent. Other countries differ. Check the treaty table in the
  account flow and confirm with a tax professional.
- Submit tax information early. Withholding applies when monthly
  earnings are finalised, not only when a payment is sent, so
  waiting for the threshold costs money.
- A missing or unapproved tax form is a payment hold. It must be
  cleared before the monthly cutoff to be paid in that cycle.
- This section is general information, not tax advice. Local tax
  law, GST or VAT, and treaty claims need a qualified professional
  in your country.

## Payment calendar and holds

- Earnings for a month are finalised in the first days of the
  following month, commonly reported as the 7th to the 12th.
- Payments go out in the 21st to 26th window when the balance has
  reached the payment threshold and no hold applies.
- The payment threshold is 100 US dollars or the local equivalent.
  The account shows the threshold in the account currency. Indian
  accounts are reported to use the US dollar thresholds. Confirm
  the figure in the account, since currency equivalents change.
- A balance below the threshold rolls forward. It is not lost.
- Payment hold causes, in order to check: missing identity
  verification, missing PIN, missing tax information, missing or
  wrong payment method, a self-set payment hold, and a policy hold.
- The account home page lists each open action. Complete them in
  the order shown and recheck after each one clears.

## When a payment is returned or delayed

1. Confirm the payment status in the account: issued, on hold, or
   returned.
2. Confirm bank details character by character with the bank.
3. Ask the bank whether it received and rejected the transfer, and
   for the rejection reason.
4. Fix the details in the payments profile, then wait for the next
   cycle. There is no way to re-trigger a single payment early.
5. Keep the transaction records. If support asks, dates and
   amounts settle the conversation.
