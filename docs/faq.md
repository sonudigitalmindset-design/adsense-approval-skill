# FAQ

Short, honest answers to the questions everyone asks.
Each answer links to the reference with the full detail.

[Back to the main page](../README.md)

## How many posts do I need before applying?

Google publishes no minimum post count. A site with few
deep, original posts beats a site with many thin ones.
What matters is value and completeness, not a number.
See
[`references/02-eligibility-and-application.md`](../references/02-eligibility-and-application.md).

## How much traffic do I need?

Google publishes no minimum traffic level. Bought traffic
hurts rather than helps. Genuine readers from search and
sharing are what matter. See
[`references/21-traffic-and-seo-basics.md`](../references/21-traffic-and-seo-basics.md).

## Does my domain need to be a certain age?

Google publishes no universal domain age rule. A new
domain with a complete, genuine site can apply. See
[`references/02-eligibility-and-application.md`](../references/02-eligibility-and-application.md).

## How long does a review take?

Usually a few days, sometimes two to four weeks. Waiting
longer than that does not mean a silent rejection. Do not
apply again while a review is open. See
[`references/02-eligibility-and-application.md`](../references/02-eligibility-and-application.md).

## Can I use AI to write my content?

Google does not ban AI-assisted content outright. It
judges helpfulness, originality, and value. Copied,
spun, or low-value content fails whatever wrote it.
Content with real experience behind it passes whatever
tool helped draft it. See
[`references/09-content-quality-guide.md`](../references/09-content-quality-guide.md).

## Can a script guarantee my approval?

No. No script can match Google's decision 100 percent.
A script checks the visible surface: pages, links, tags,
and files. The decision also weighs content value,
originality, traffic quality, and account history, which
no crawler can see. See
[`references/31-approval-check-matrix.md`](../references/31-approval-check-matrix.md).

## What does "low value content" mean?

The most common rejection reason. It means the reviewer
found the content thin, repetitive, copied, or adding
nothing beyond what every other site says. The fix is
deeper content with real experience, not more words.
See
[`references/07-rejection-reasons-and-fixes.md`](../references/07-rejection-reasons-and-fixes.md).

## What is an ad serving limit?

A temporary restriction while Google assesses the account
or investigates invalid traffic concerns. It typically
lasts less than 30 days but can take longer. The account
stays accessible, and the review is automatic. There is
no appeal form or manual-lift request for limits. See
[`references/24-ad-serving-limits-and-enforcement-levels.md`](../references/24-ad-serving-limits-and-enforcement-levels.md).

## Do I need a Privacy Policy, About page, and Contact page?

Yes, in practice. They are the trust signals every
reviewer looks for. The Privacy Policy must describe
cookies, Google advertising, third parties, and opt-out
options. See
[`references/08-pre-apply-checklist.md`](../references/08-pre-apply-checklist.md)
and
[`examples/privacy-policy-outline.md`](../examples/privacy-policy-outline.md).

## Do I need a consent banner?

For visitors from the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, yes:
a certified consent platform is required, and Google's
free option is Funding Choices. See
[`references/18-consent-and-privacy-deep-dive.md`](../references/18-consent-and-privacy-deep-dive.md).

## Can I have more than one AdSense account?

One person or organisation may hold one AdSense account.
Opening a second account after a closure is a policy
violation. See
[`references/27-account-architecture-and-graduation.md`](../references/27-account-architecture-and-graduation.md).

## My account was closed. Can I appeal?

It depends on the closure reason. Invalid traffic
closures have one online appeal form, and further appeals
may not be considered after a decision. A refused appeal
means no new account. Read the notice first, then
[`references/15-policy-center-and-appeals.md`](../references/15-policy-center-and-appeals.md).
