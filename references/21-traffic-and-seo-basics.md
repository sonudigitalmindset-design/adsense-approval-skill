# Traffic and SEO basics

How to build the legitimate traffic AdSense needs, and how search
visibility supports approval and earnings. No traffic number is
required for approval, and no timeline is promised. The rule is
simple: real people, finding real content, for real reasons.

## Traffic Google trusts

- Search traffic: people finding pages through Google Search.
  The most durable source for most content sites.
- Direct traffic: people typing the address or using a bookmark.
  Grows with brand and repeat value.
- Referral traffic: links from other real sites, newsletters, and
  communities.
- Social traffic: people following links shared on social
  platforms. Real shares by real accounts.
- Email traffic: readers from a newsletter they chose to join.

## Traffic that puts the account at risk

- Bought traffic from traffic sellers, click exchanges, and
  pay-to-click services.
- Bots, auto-refresh scripts, and traffic from hacked or spoofed
  sources.
- Misleading social posts that send people to ad-heavy pages with
  no matching content.
- Popups and redirects that push visitors to pages they did not
  choose.
- Any scheme that pays, rewards, or pressures people to visit or
  click.

If traffic arrives from an unknown source, investigate before
celebrating. A spike that is not real can cost the account. See
`05-program-policies-ads-traffic.md`.

## Search Console fundamentals

- Verify the site in Google Search Console. A domain property
  covers all subdomains and protocols.
- Submit the sitemap. Confirm Search Console accepts it and
  reports discovered URLs.
- Read the Pages report: indexed pages, and the reasons for pages
  not indexed. Fix real errors such as 404s and server errors.
  Understand that some exclusions (such as duplicate or
  low-value URLs) are normal.
- Request indexing for important new or fixed pages, one at a
  time. Do not request indexing for the whole site daily.
- Check the Search results report for queries and pages that get
  impressions. Impressions before clicks are normal for a new
  site.
- Keep Search Console connected in the SEO plugin where possible,
  so the site owner sees the same data Google sees.

## On-page SEO that also helps approval

- One clear topic per page, answered fully. A page that answers
  its title question beats a page that circles it.
- Descriptive titles and meta descriptions, one per page, written
  for humans. No keyword stuffing.
- One H1 per page, with headings that outline the answer.
- Internal links between related posts, using honest anchor text.
  Help readers and crawlers find the next useful page.
- Descriptive image file names and alt text that say what the
  image shows.
- Fast loading: compressed images, minimal heavy scripts, and a
  caching setup that actually works.
- Mobile-first layout: readable text, tappable targets, and no
  horizontal scrolling.
- HTTPS everywhere, with no mixed content warnings.

## Content strategy for durable traffic

- Write for questions people actually ask, in the words they use.
  Search Console queries and community questions are better topic
  sources than keyword tools alone.
- Cover a topic cluster, not scattered one-off posts. A cluster of
  related, interlinked posts builds topical trust.
- Update old posts when facts change. A dated guide that is still
  accurate earns more than a new guide that is wrong.
- Publish on a steady rhythm the writer can sustain. Regularity
  beats bursts followed by silence.
- Promote honestly: share where the audience already gathers, with
  a description that matches the page.
- Build an email list with consent. Repeat readers are the most
  stable traffic a site can own.

## Page experience

- Core Web Vitals measure loading, interactivity, and visual
  stability. Poor scores lose readers before ads load.
- Common fixes: smaller images, fewer third-party scripts, no
  layout shifts from late-loading ads or fonts, and a fast host.
- Test real pages on a real phone on a mobile connection, not only
  on a fast desktop.
- Intrusive interstitials that block content hurt both search and
  ad policy. Keep entry popups minimal and dismissible.

## What not to chase

- Do not chase a traffic number before applying. Google publishes
  no minimum, and junk traffic is worse than little traffic.
- Do not chase backlinks from link farms or paid link schemes.
  Bad links hurt search trust.
- Do not copy competitors' posts to match their traffic. Original
  work compounds. Copies get filtered.
- Do not measure success by impressions alone. Earnings follow
  engaged readers, not raw page views.

## A realistic first-year picture

- Months of steady publishing usually pass before search traffic
  becomes meaningful. That is normal.
- Early traffic often comes from sharing and communities while
  search visibility builds.
- The sites that get approved and stay approved treat AdSense as
  the reward for a useful site, not as the reason the site exists.
