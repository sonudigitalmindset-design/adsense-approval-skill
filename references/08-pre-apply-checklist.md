# Pre-apply checklist

Work through this list before the first application and again before
every reapplication. Tick an item only when you have verified it on
the live site, not when you believe it is done.

## Identity and ownership

- [ ] Applicant is 18 or over, or a parent/guardian is applying with
      their own Google Account.
- [ ] The applicant owns or controls the site and can edit its HTML.
- [ ] No second AdSense account exists for the same payee.
- [ ] The payment country is correct. It generally cannot be changed
      later.
- [ ] The site is in a country/territory and language AdSense supports.

## Site readiness

- [ ] The site is public, loads over HTTPS, and has no password wall
      or maintenance mode.
- [ ] The homepage clearly explains what the site is about.
- [ ] Navigation works on mobile and desktop. Every menu link opens a
      real page.
- [ ] No broken links, no empty categories, no demo content, no
      "Hello world" post, no placeholder text.
- [ ] robots.txt does not block Googlebot, and a sitemap exists.
- [ ] Pages load in a reasonable time and text is readable on a phone.
- [ ] No pop-ups cover the content on first load. No auto-playing
      video with sound blocks reading.

## Trust pages

- [ ] About page: who runs the site, what it covers, and why readers
      should trust it. Real names and a real story, in your own words.
- [ ] Contact page: a working contact form or a monitored email
      address, tested end to end.
- [ ] Privacy Policy: what data is collected, cookies and advertising
      disclosure naming Google and third parties, how users can manage
      ad personalisation, and contact details. Written for this site,
      not pasted unchanged from a template.
- [ ] Terms page where the niche needs it, with the governing
      jurisdiction stated honestly.
- [ ] Disclaimer where the niche needs it (health, finance, legal):
      the content is general information, not professional advice, and
      readers should consult a qualified professional.
- [ ] All trust pages are linked from the header or footer on every
      screen size, and each one shows a "last updated" date.

## Content

- [ ] Every indexed post is original, complete, and genuinely useful.
      Each one fully answers its question.
- [ ] No copied, scraped, spun, or unedited auto-generated pages.
- [ ] No invented numbers, fake quotes, or claims presented as tested
      when they were not tested.
- [ ] Health, finance, and legal claims carry credible sources, and
      risky advice is qualified honestly.
- [ ] Every image is owned, licensed, or properly free to use, with
      credit where the licence requires it. No images taken from search
      results.
- [ ] Every post has a real author name and, where the theme supports
      it, an author bio.
- [ ] Categories and tags are real, populated, and not duplicated.
      Empty tag pages are noindexed or removed.
- [ ] Comments are moderated. Spam, links to prohibited content, and
      abusive comments are removed.
- [ ] Affiliate content is a minor part of the site unless it adds
      original review, testing, or comparison value, and affiliate
      relationships are disclosed.

## Policy audit

- [ ] Whole site checked against `03-publisher-policies.md`
      (prohibited content and behaviour).
- [ ] Whole site checked against `04-publisher-restrictions.md`
      (restricted content). Restricted pages are isolated and few.
- [ ] No traffic from paid-to-click, exchange, autosurf, or bot
      sources. No click requests anywhere on the site.
- [ ] No ads placed yet in misleading positions, and no plan to place
      ads next to download or play buttons.
- [ ] Consent banner in place where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland),
      using a Google-certified CMP.

## Technical setup for the application

- [ ] AdSense verification code placed between `<head>` tags on pages
      that get regular views, or verification completed by ads.txt or
      meta tag.
- [ ] ads.txt published at the site root and verified at the live URL.
- [ ] Search Console property verified and the sitemap submitted, so
      indexing can be checked (recommended, not required).
- [ ] A note made of the application date, so the review window
      (usually a few days, sometimes 2 to 4 weeks) can be tracked.

## Verdict

- Apply only when every blocker item above is verified. If any item is
  unchecked, fix it first. A rushed application costs more time than a
  careful one, because each rejection restarts the review clock.
