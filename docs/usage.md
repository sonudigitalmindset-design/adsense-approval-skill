# How to use this skill

Workflows for the three situations this skill exists for:
preparing a first site, fixing a rejection, and keeping an
approved account safe.

[Back to the main page](../README.md)

## Preparing a first site

1. Read [Understanding AdSense approval](understanding.md).
2. Learn the vocabulary in
   [`references/10-glossary.md`](../references/10-glossary.md).
3. Check the eligibility rules and the myths in
   [`references/02-eligibility-and-application.md`](../references/02-eligibility-and-application.md).
4. Read the policy layers in
   [`references/03-publisher-policies.md`](../references/03-publisher-policies.md),
   [`references/04-publisher-restrictions.md`](../references/04-publisher-restrictions.md),
   and
   [`references/05-program-policies-ads-traffic.md`](../references/05-program-policies-ads-traffic.md).
5. Write content that passes, following
   [`references/09-content-quality-guide.md`](../references/09-content-quality-guide.md).
6. Run the quick checker, then the deep audit. See
   [Audit scripts](audit-scripts.md).
7. Complete the human checklist in
   [`references/08-pre-apply-checklist.md`](../references/08-pre-apply-checklist.md).
8. Apply. Do not apply the same day you fix the last
   issue. Let the site settle, then apply once.

## Fixing a rejection

1. Find the exact rejection reason in the notice. Do not
   guess. The reason in the notice is the only reason
   that matters.
2. Look it up in
   [`references/07-rejection-reasons-and-fixes.md`](../references/07-rejection-reasons-and-fixes.md)
   and follow its fix plan.
3. Check the 52 mistakes in
   [`references/12-mistakes-encyclopedia.md`](../references/12-mistakes-encyclopedia.md)
   for anything the notice did not name.
4. Re-run the deep audit and fix every failure. See
   [Audit scripts](audit-scripts.md).
5. Fix the site properly, wait, and reapply once. Repeated
   applications without real fixes do not help.
6. If the case involves a policy enforcement, read
   [`references/15-policy-center-and-appeals.md`](../references/15-policy-center-and-appeals.md)
   before writing anything to Google.

## Running an approved account

1. Learn the ad formats and placement rules in
   [`references/13-ad-formats-and-placement.md`](../references/13-ad-formats-and-placement.md).
2. Set up consent correctly with
   [`references/18-consent-and-privacy-deep-dive.md`](../references/18-consent-and-privacy-deep-dive.md).
3. Optimise honestly with
   [`references/14-optimization-and-revenue.md`](../references/14-optimization-and-revenue.md).
   Never click your own ads, never ask others to click,
   and never buy traffic.
4. Defend against invalid traffic with
   [`references/25-invalid-traffic-defense.md`](../references/25-invalid-traffic-defense.md).
5. Run the monthly review in
   [`examples/advanced-monthly-review-checklist.md`](../examples/advanced-monthly-review-checklist.md).
6. For hard situations, use the ten playbooks in
   [`references/30-advanced-decision-playbooks.md`](../references/30-advanced-decision-playbooks.md).

## Asking your assistant

When the skill is installed, ask in plain words. Good
questions name the site, the situation, and the exact
notice or message:

- "Audit https://example.com for AdSense approval."
- "My site was rejected for low value content. What do I
  fix first?"
- "Explain ad serving limits and what I should do during
  one."
- "What does my Policy Center message mean, and what is
  the fix workflow?"

The skill's instructions live in `SKILL.md`. The workflows
above follow them. If the assistant invents a rule, ask it
to show the official source. Live official pages win over
this skill. The full link list is in
[`references/11-official-sources.md`](../references/11-official-sources.md).
